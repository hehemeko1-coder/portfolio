01 / ABOUT ME

Creative by nature.
Building by practice.

I’m a self-taught multidisciplinary creative based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

My work sits between visual communication and technology. I’m developing my graphic design practice through posters, social media visuals, typography, and layout, while building websites and exploring user experience, front-end development, and practical digital tools.

I value clarity, strong visual hierarchy, accessible interfaces, and honest learning. I document what I make, what I’m improving, and what I’m ready to take on.
